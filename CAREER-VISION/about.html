About Career Vision

Helping students choose their path with clarity and confidence

WHAT IS CAREER VISION?

A student-first career guidance platform

Career Vision is a student-focused career guidance website built as a Community Service Project. It brings together career paths, education routes and learning resources for students at every stage — after 10th, after Intermediate and after Graduation.

Why We Created It

Many students struggle to find clear, organised information about the career options available to them. Decisions are often made based on incomplete information or peer pressure rather than a real understanding of the paths ahead.

The Problem Students Face

Scattered information, limited awareness of alternatives, unreliable resources and no single place to compare career paths side by side.

Our Solution

A simple, organised website covering every education stage with clear explanations, curated resources and a consistent, easy-to-navigate design.

Who Can Use It

Students after 10th choosing a stream, students after Intermediate choosing a degree, and graduates planning their next step.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Built with clear goals in mind

This project was created as part of our Community Service Project, with the following goals.

Simplify Choices

Present career paths in a clear, structured way.

Support Students

Give students a reliable starting point for research.

Share Resources

Point students to real, useful learning material.

Community Impact

Serve as a small, practical contribution to student communities.
