Merchant Navy

A seafaring career in marine engineering or nautical science

Eligibility10+2 with PCM
CoursesMarine Engg, Nautical Science
TrainingPre-sea + Onboard Training
FieldShipping & Maritime

Overview

Merchant Navy careers involve working on cargo and passenger ships that transport goods across the world. Entry is typically through Marine Engineering or Nautical Science courses after Intermediate.
