Polytechnic Education

Diploma pathways through polytechnic institutes

What is Polytechnic Education?

Polytechnic institutes offer 3-year diploma programmes in engineering and technical fields, admitting students directly after 10th. They combine classroom learning with hands-on lab and workshop training.
