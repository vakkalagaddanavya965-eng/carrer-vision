NDA — National Defence Academy

A path into the Indian Armed Forces after Intermediate

Eligibility10+2, unmarried, per notification
SelectionWritten Exam + SSB Interview
Training3 Years at NDA
ServicesArmy, Navy, Air Force

Overview

NDA is a joint defence service academy where cadets train together before being commissioned into the Army, Navy or Air Force. Entry is through a national-level written examination followed by an SSB interview.

Selection Process

UPSC Written ExamSSB InterviewMedical Examination

Training

Career Opportunities

Eligibility criteria, age limits and vacancies change with each notification. Always verify current details on the official UPSC and NDA websites before applying.
