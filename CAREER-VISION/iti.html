ITI (Industrial Training Institute)

Short, hands-on trade courses for skilled technical jobs

Duration6 Months – 2 Years
EligibilityPass in 8th/10th
CertificationNCVT / SCVT
FocusPractical trade skills

Electrician

Wiring, maintenance and installation of electrical systems.

Fitter

Assembling and maintaining mechanical components and machines.

Welder

Joining metal parts using various welding techniques.

Mechanic

Repair and maintenance of vehicles and engines.

Computer Operator

Basic computer operations, typing and office software.

Plumber

Installation and repair of water supply and drainage systems.

Electronics Mechanic

Repair and assembly of electronic devices and circuits.

After ITI, students can pursue apprenticeships, appear for government technical posts, or continue into a diploma for further education.
