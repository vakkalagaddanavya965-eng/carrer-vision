Commercial Pilot

Train to fly and build a career in commercial aviation

Eligibility10+2 with Physics & Maths
LicenceCPL from DGCA
Training DurationApprox. 18–24 Months
FieldCommercial Aviation

Overview

Becoming a commercial pilot involves flight training at a DGCA-approved flying school, followed by ground and simulator exams to earn a Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL).
